01 / THE WHY

Less confusion. More clarity.

A great project deserves a better starting point.

Project requirements often begin as scattered conversations, messages, and incomplete questionnaires.

Important details can be missed, leading to repeated clarification and unnecessary revisions.

Elunette was created to make this process simpler through guided questions, completeness checking, and organised reviews.
